Ultimate Updater lists the updates available from many sources and starts the ones you select. It also includes a cleanup feature that can remove temporary files, caches and orphaned registry entries.

1. Updates

Updates are only ever started through the official mechanism of the source they come from (winget, Windows Update, Chocolatey, Scoop, pip, npm, .NET tools, the PowerShell Gallery, Steam, and the vendor pages it links to). Ultimate Updater does not review, host or modify the packages, installers or update contents of those sources.

You decide which sources are enabled and what gets installed, and you are responsible for trusting the sources and packages you choose.

2. Cleanup

The cleanup deletes the contents of temporary directories and caches, empties the recycle bin if you ask it to, removes orphaned registry entries, and starts Windows' own tools (Disk Cleanup, sfc, DISM). You decide before every run which areas are included.

Deleted data is gone for good. The one exception is leftover program folders, which are moved to the recycle bin. Before registry entries are removed, a backup of the affected areas is written; no guarantee is given that it is complete or that a restore will succeed.

Back up anything you care about before starting a cleanup. No liability is accepted for deleted files, lost settings, programs that no longer start or no longer work correctly after a cleanup, the effects of the registry cleanup, or the consequences of the repair and cleanup steps Windows itself performs.
